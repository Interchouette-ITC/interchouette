Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Interchouette - ITC (“we”, “us”, or “our”), operated by Gregory Roussac, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our websites, applications, and related services (the “Services”), including integrations with third-party platforms such as LinkedIn.

1. Data we collect

Depending on how you interact with the Services, we may collect:

  • Account and profile data you provide or authorize (e.g. name, email address, profile picture, LinkedIn profile identifiers).
  • Authentication data when you connect via OAuth or similar providers (access tokens, refresh tokens, and related metadata).
  • Usage data such as pages visited, features used, timestamps, and approximate technical information (browser type, device, IP address), including via analytics if you accept non-essential cookies.
  • Visitor chat data when you use the site chat: message text, optional email you leave for follow-up, and technical session identifiers needed to keep the conversation going.
  • Communications you send us (support requests, emails).

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data beyond what is needed to operate the Services or what you explicitly choose to share.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We distinguish essential storage (needed for the site and chat to work) from non-essential cookies used for analytics.

  • Google Analytics 4 (measurement id on this site) may set cookies / identifiers to understand aggregated traffic. It loads only after you Accept non-essential cookies in the consent banner. If you Decline, we do not load that analytics tag for your browser.
  • Chat storage in your browser ( localStorage / sessionStorage ) keeps a short chat resume token, recent messages (about seven days), and any email you chose to save in the chat form so it can reappear when you open that form again.
  • Consent choice itself is stored locally so we remember Accept or Decline.

3. Visitor chat, Slack, and optional email

When chat is available, messages may be relayed to Gregory Roussac over Slack when he is online. When he is away, answers may be generated from public Interchouette notes (including via Interchouette MCP) and may use OpenRouter for language-model replies. If you leave an email in chat, we use it only so Greg can follow up; it is also kept in your browser storage as above until you clear site data.

4. Why we collect data

We use personal data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Services;
  • Authenticate users and manage connected accounts (including LinkedIn);
  • Operate visitor chat and optional follow-up by email;
  • Respond to requests and provide support;
  • Monitor security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations;
  • Understand aggregated usage (analytics) when you have accepted non-essential cookies.

5. How we use and share data

We process data only for the purposes above. We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers that help us host, secure, or operate the Services (under appropriate confidentiality obligations), including hosting and (if accepted) analytics;
  • Slack when live chat relays a visitor message to Greg’s workspace;
  • OpenRouter when away-mode chat uses a language model (message text is sent to generate a reply);
  • Platforms you connect (e.g. LinkedIn), according to their terms and the permissions you grant;
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

6. LinkedIn integration

If you authorize LinkedIn access, we receive only the information permitted by LinkedIn’s APIs and your consent. We use that information solely to provide the features you requested. You can revoke access at any time from your LinkedIn account settings and/or by contacting us.

7. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. In particular:

  • Browser chat transcript cache is intended to expire after about seven days (or sooner if you clear site data);
  • Slack chat threads and optional follow-up email are kept as long as needed to respond and operate the Services, then deleted or minimized when no longer needed;
  • Analytics data (if you accepted cookies) follows the retention settings of the analytics provider and our configuration.

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

8. Your rights and deletion requests

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of your personal data;
  • Withdraw consent or disconnect third-party integrations.

To request access, correction, or deletion, email contact@interchouette.net with the subject “Privacy request”. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Services after changes means you accept the updated policy.
