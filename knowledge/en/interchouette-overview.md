# Interchouette ITC (English)

Interchouette (Interchouette - ITC) is a Dutch sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak / ZZP) specializing in custom IT software development, based in Hilversum, the Netherlands. The firm was founded in 2022 by senior software engineer **Gregory Roussac**.

Through https://interchouette.net/ Gregory Roussac offers freelance development services under the Interchouette brand.
